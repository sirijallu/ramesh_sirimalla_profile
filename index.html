About Me

I'm a Data & AI leader with over two decades of experience building and running enterprise-scale data, cloud, and artificial intelligence platforms. My career has taken me from hands-on data engineering to leading global technology organizations, always with the same focus: translating data into decisions and AI into measurable business value.

I've led cloud migrations spanning hundreds of applications, stood up enterprise Generative AI Centers of Excellence, and built data platforms that support thousands of analysts and decision-makers. I care deeply about responsible, well-governed AI, pragmatic architecture, and growing the next generation of technical leaders.

When I'm not architecting platforms or mentoring teams, I write and speak about enterprise AI adoption, cloud strategy, and building high-performing data organizations.

I lead by pairing a clear long-term vision with hands-on technical depth. I set ambitious goals, give teams the context and autonomy to own them, and stay close enough to the architecture to make sound trade-offs quickly. I believe the best data and AI organizations are built on trust, transparency, and a bias for shipping real business outcomes.

Key Leadership Principles

🎯

Strategic Thinking

Connect data and AI investments directly to business outcomes, and plan for where the organization needs to be in three years—not just next quarter.

🔗

Systems Thinking

Look at platforms, processes, and people as one system, so a change in one area strengthens rather than breaks another.

🤝

Servant Leadership

Remove blockers, protect focus, and invest in each person's growth so teams can do the best work of their careers.

📊

Data-Driven Decisions

Use metrics and evidence—not opinions—to prioritize work, measure impact, and course-correct early.

🛡️

Responsible Innovation

Move fast with AI while building in governance, security, and ethics from day one rather than bolting them on later.

🌱

Growing Leaders

Mentor engineers into senior and leadership roles, and build succession into every team so success outlasts any one person.

How I Apply Them

🧭

Clarity First

Every initiative starts with a clear problem statement, success metrics, and an owner.

🚀

Deliver Incrementally

Ship value in small, visible milestones instead of long programs that report progress only at the end.

💬

Open Communication

Share context openly, invite dissent, and explain the "why" behind decisions to executives and engineers alike.

🔁

Continuous Improvement

Run blameless retrospectives and treat every setback as input for a better next iteration.

Tech Strategy

Five pillars I use to design, modernize, and run enterprise data and AI platforms that stay reliable, secure, and cost-efficient as they scale.

🧱
Pillar 1

Modern Data Foundation

A single, governed lakehouse as the source of truth.

  • Unify warehouse and lake workloads
  • Model data once, reuse everywhere
  • Support batch and streaming side by side
🛡️
Pillar 2

Governance & Security

Trust built in, not bolted on.

  • Fine-grained access controls and lineage
  • Data quality checks in every pipeline
  • Encryption and auditability by default
🤖
Pillar 3

Responsible AI at Scale

From pilots to production, safely.

  • Reusable GenAI and MLOps platforms
  • Model evaluation, monitoring, and guardrails
  • Clear AI policies and review processes
💰
Pillar 4

Cost Optimization

Every dollar of cloud spend tied to value.

  • FinOps practices and showback
  • Right-size compute and storage
  • Retire legacy platforms decisively
⚙️
Pillar 5

Operational Excellence

Platforms that run themselves.

  • Infrastructure as code and CI/CD
  • Observability with clear SLAs
  • Automate recovery and repetitive toil

Why it matters

Strong foundations let organizations adopt AI quickly without trading away trust, reliability, or cost control.

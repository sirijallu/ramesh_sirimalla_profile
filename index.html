About Me

I'm a Forward Deployed Engineer at CVS Health with 15+ years of experience building production-grade real-time data platforms, APIs, and Generative AI systems across healthcare, financial services, and public-sector programs. I apply advanced data engineering to develop scalable, reliable solutions — focusing on data ingestion, processing, and streaming pipelines.

My work centers on Apache Kafka, Apache Flink, Spark, and the Spring ecosystem: from real-time notifications that reach patients across SMS, email, push, and IVR, to Tier 1 orchestration APIs at CVS Health, to payment-transaction pipelines for Zelle at Early Warning. I'm experienced in architecting multi-agent workflows and enterprise RAG systems, and in working directly with business and third-party partners to take solutions into production.

I enjoy solving complex data challenges, mentoring engineers on best practices, and delivering customer-centric outcomes that align with business goals.

I lead from the code outward. I stay hands-on in design and implementation, embed with business and third-party partners to understand the real problem, and help my team ship production-ready solutions — not just demos. I believe reliable systems come from clear ownership, strong engineering practices, and open collaboration.

Tech Strategy

Five pillars I use to design real-time data platforms and GenAI systems that hold up in production — in regulated industries like healthcare and payments.

🎯
Pillar 1

Reliable Delivery

Every message, exactly when it matters.

  • Deduplicate and join streams with KStreams
  • Meet business and legal delivery windows
  • Design for retries and failure from day one
🧩
Pillar 2

Event-Driven Decoupling

Loosely coupled services that scale independently.

  • Kafka, Redpanda, and Azure Service Bus as the backbone
  • Clean RESTful APIs at the edges
  • Stateful processing in Kafka Streams and Flink
📟
Pillar 3

Observability

Know before your users do.

  • Splunk and Grafana dashboards for business and engineering
  • High and critical alerting for Tier 1 systems
  • Real-time visibility into transactions
🛡️
Pillar 4

Security & Compliance

Built for healthcare and financial data.

  • Access control and audit trails
  • PII detection and redaction
  • Code reviews against industry regulations
🤖
Pillar 5

Production-Grade GenAI

From prototype to trusted system.

  • RAG and multi-agent workflows with LangGraph
  • LLM evaluation and guardrails
  • Prompt-injection mitigation and structured outputs

Why it matters

Real-time systems touch patients, customers, and payments directly. Getting reliability, observability, and compliance right is what turns a streaming pipeline into a service people can trust.
